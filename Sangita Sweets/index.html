The Sangita story

Keeping Bihar's sweet tradition on the table.

Sangita is a traditional sweet shop on Buddh Marg, opposite Patna Museum, known for Bihari favourites such as khaja, anarsa and gujia, alongside laddus and savoury snacks.

Public business listings describe the shop as a long-running Patna business with 52+ years in business. Rather than inventing a founding year, this site uses the verified “52+ years” description.
